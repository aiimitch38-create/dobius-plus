## Gotcha discovered (skill improvement)
Churn ranking counts **deleted** files. The #1, #3, #5, #6 entries by raw churn
(`src-tauri/src/lib.rs`, `Cargo.toml`, `tauri.conf.json`, `managed_agents/runtime.rs`)
do not exist at HEAD. Ranking must be filtered to live files or it points at ghosts.
