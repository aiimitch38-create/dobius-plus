# ADAM control layer and Dobius execution layer

Carson clarified on 2026-10-14: “think of Dobius as more like the execution layer and Adam is the real like control layer on top of everything.” This is the controlling architecture for the active non-AWS build.

ADAM operating system owns goals, priorities, approved plans, permissions, resource budgets, routing, cancellation and recovery decisions. It maintains the control job/intent record and stable approval identity. Dobius executes authorized jobs on registered laptop/VPS workers, checks local permissions/capacity and lease validity, maintains execution evidence, and returns ordered durable results to ADAM. Local safety checks do not make Dobius the planning authority.

The intended flow is: user/phone request -> ADAM planning and approval -> durable authorized job/intent -> authenticated Dobius execution -> durable events/results -> ADAM reconciliation -> Kanban projection. Kanban uses that same linked job lifecycle, not a separate independently writable queue. Control records and executor receipts may occupy separate databases; they must preserve stable IDs, immutable approved payload digests, deduplication and authoritative ownership for each transition.

ADAM remains independent of Dobius app/worker lifecycle, including independent or remote hosting. The current caller-preflight proposal to make ADAM itself a Dobius-main-owned service child is superseded as the final architecture. A bounded execution-side adapter can be owned by Dobius without moving ADAM's control service underneath the executor.

The live bridge is absent. Existing local runtime token possession, authenticatedLocalSocket, a hard-coded planner principal, paths/file hashes and injected callbacks do not establish an independent ADAM service identity. The next bridge must authenticate the actual ADAM service and bind approved jobs to its registered instance and current authorization epoch, with revocation, bounded framing, deadlines and replay protection. This source contract does not approve unrestricted same-user unseal access or provision new credentials. Secret decryption remains internal and unexposed until an appropriate caller boundary is reviewed.

Dobius main can own its local OS-backed capability encryption implementation. That is execution plumbing, not control authority. Local and remote key custody must be explicit; a remote ADAM service cannot be assumed to possess local Electron safeStorage access. Exact matching consumed-grant replay/child lookup after expiry must remain possible; freshness and current approval govern new submission, without renewing a grant or creating a duplicate child to mask an ambiguous result.
